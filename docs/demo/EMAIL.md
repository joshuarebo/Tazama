## Notes for you, not for the email

- The four tabs are **Why it exists**, **How it works**, **What it looks like**, **Going live**.
  The second one is the interactive bit: seven steps, click through them.
- **Do not** say the accuracy figures predict live performance. They measure the mechanism on
  made up cases.
- **If he asks about predictive maintenance:** the three scenarios are on the last tab. The
  honest line is that today the product spots a fault early, and predicting one needs
  readings we do not collect yet. The first step is the site power feed.
- **If he asks how long to go live:** connecting the data is configuration, not new code. But
  three privacy steps have to be done before any real customer record enters the system.
- **If he asks whether the AI can break something:** no. It recommends. It has no ability to
  change anything on the network, and that is by design rather than by policy.
